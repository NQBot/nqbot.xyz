Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

NQBot LIVE (nqbotlive.com) is an educational futures-market livestream with AI hosts, paper-trading bots and a public scoreboard. This page explains what information the website, the livestream chat, and the "NQ Stream Bot" Google application handle.

This website

The website is a static site. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no forms, and no advertising or analytics scripts, and it does not set its own cookies. The home page embeds the YouTube player, and YouTube may set cookies or collect data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Livestream chat

During livestreams we read the public live chat on our YouTube channel. For each message we receive the public display name, the channel ID, and the message text. We use these to answer viewer questions on stream, to greet returning viewers by a shortened version of their display name, and to see which questions and topics viewers care about. Messages and the replies we give are kept in log files on the operator's own computer. We do not sell this information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

When a question is answered, the message text is sent to a third-party AI model provider so that a reply can be written. We do not send any other personal information with it.

To have your messages removed from our logs, or to ask for a different spoken name, email the address at the bottom of this page, or tell the hosts in chat ("call me ...").

The "NQ Stream Bot" Google application

NQ Stream Bot is a private application used only by the owner of the NQBot LIVE YouTube channel. It uses the YouTube API, signed in as the channel owner, for three purposes: reading our own channel's live chat, posting replies into that chat, and managing our own livestream broadcasts. It does not access the Google account, YouTube data, or other personal data of viewers or any other user.

The sign-in token for the application is stored in an encrypted credential store on the operator's computer. It is not transmitted to anyone else, and it is used only for the purposes above.

NQBot LIVE's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

This site and stream are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
