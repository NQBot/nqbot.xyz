How to read the table below

Small samples lie. With ten to twenty-four attempts apiece, the gap between a 46% strategy and a 37% one is not a ranking — it is noise. The three published above have overlapping confidence intervals, and several strategies further down the table are statistically indistinguishable from them too. They were chosen for having a decent rate and a real sample, one per session, not because the ordering is meaningful.

The top of the table is the trap. The strategy showing 100% passed a single attempt. Anything with fewer than about ten attempts tells you almost nothing, in either direction.

A pass rate is not an edge. What produces passes here is a modest geometric edge, hard discipline rules, and an account guard that stops the moment the target is banked. The same Donchian logic run on the Asian session instead of the US one passed 1 attempt in 17 — identical rules, different hours, near-total failure.

What is the Eval Arena?

Prop-firm evaluations are how real traders earn funded accounts: grow a $50,000 account by a set profit target without ever letting the balance fall below a liquidation line that trails your best equity. The Eval Arena runs that exact gauntlet — but the contestants are our trading algorithms, and the money is entirely simulated. Each strategy from the live broadcast is assigned its own arena account and trades it 23 hours a day, 5 days a week, under the same rules a human would face.

No cherry-picking, no do-overs: outcomes are scored by deterministic replay of the actual NQ price tape, so every strategy is judged against the same market. When a strategy passes, it goes on the record and starts a fresh attempt. When it blows the drawdown, that goes on the record too. Over time the arena tells us — and you — which strategies could actually survive a funded-account evaluation, and which just look good on a backtest.

Shadow Balance

The strategy's current simulated account balance on this attempt. It starts at $50,000 and every simulated fill, win or lose, moves it — exactly like a real eval account statement.

To Target

Dollars remaining between the shadow balance and the $53,000 pass line. When this hits zero, the strategy officially passes the attempt.

Pass / Fail Record

Completed attempts only. A pass means the target was reached; a fail means the trailing liquidation line was hit. In-progress attempts don't count until they finish.

Reading the colors — same code as the Chart Director scoreboard on the live channel: Green = Pass Red = Fail Yellow = Halted White = Active Grey = Inactive
